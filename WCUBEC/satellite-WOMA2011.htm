Satellite Workshop to WOMA 2011



General Information

Satellite Workshop to WOMA 2011 is a small workshop proceding the main event WOMA 2011. The theme of this satellite workshop is "Novel Processes in Microcavities". The purpose of the workshop is to introduce the recent advances in microcavities to  researchers and students in the Seoul area who may not be able to attend the WOMA 2011 in Pusan. But anyones who are interested in the workshop are cordially invited to attend the workshop.


Venue

Satellite Workshop to WOMA 2011 is held at Rm. 23-417, Department of Physics and Astronomy, Seoul National University on May 23, 2011.


Registration

There is no registration fee. A laboratory tour  is planned in the afternoon of May 23, 2011. If you want to join the tour, please sign up at the registration desk.
