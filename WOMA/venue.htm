BK Auditorium (501-101)

Building 501 (BK building of the College of Natural Science)                       BK Auditorium (Mokam hall, 501-101)

    

* If you stay at Hoam Faculty House, take the town bus No. 2 across the street, get off at Beo-Dul-Gol (or College of Education) and walk to the venue (10 min). Bus fare is 700 Won. The driver takes a 1000-Won bill. If you come from other places, use the subway line No. 2 and the bus No. 5513. See the transportation section for details.
