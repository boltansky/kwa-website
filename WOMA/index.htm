Post-Workshop Message from Organizers


Dear WOMA2009 Participants,

We organizers of WOMA2009 would like to thank all participants for coming to Seoul and presenting stimulating talks and posters at WOMA2009. We all witnessed many up-to-date and cutting edge research results presented during the workshop. In particular, the question and comment sessions following each talk were very active and stimulating. All of these were not possible without your active participation and contribution.

We have posted the pdf copies of the presentations at 'Scientific Program' menu for your information. The files are for the WOMA participants only and thus are encrypted with a password, which has been sent to you already. For more information on downloading the files, please contact woma(at)woma.snu.ac.kr.

We also posted many pictures taken during the workshop at a new menu 'Photos'.

Many people expressed their view on this workshop in one way or another. Many suggested to continue this unique workshop which brings together the world leading scientists in the field of experimental and theoretical micro- and nano-cavities. We the organizing committee members thinks the same and we will definitely come back and have the second WOMA, probably in 2011.

We would like to thank you all again for having made this workshop a great success. We hope to see you again at another WOMA.

Best Regards,

Organizers
